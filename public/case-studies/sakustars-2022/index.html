01 / Project context

A coherent identity for a national event.

SAKUstars brings vocational students together to celebrate creativity and cultural skills. The 2022 competition took place in Tuusula on 26–28 April under the theme Vanhassa vara parempi (“Reusing the old is better”).

The design needed to work at many scales: on the event website, on-screen during ceremonies, in social and print communications and on physical event materials.

My original owl illustration was selected as the event logo and became the common visual element linking these touchpoints.

02 / What I designed

One identity, many applications.

Visual identity

Illustrated the owl logo and extended the concept into posters, banners, roll-ups, postcards, badges, stickers, clothing prints and event graphics.

Website & UI/UX

Designed and developed the 2022 event website in WordPress, combining a recognisable visual language with usable content, navigation and page layouts.

Motion & presentation

Created animations and ceremony-related materials to carry the identity into screen-based event moments.

03 / Design approach

Graphic consistency meets usability.

My responsibilities connected graphic design with functional web design. I developed a reusable visual direction across physical and digital media, then brought that identity into the WordPress experience.

Usability evaluation was part of the web-design work, helping ensure the event website served real visitors rather than functioning only as a promotional graphic. The case highlights both visual craft and attention to how people access information.

The central design decision was to make the event recognisable wherever people encountered it—from a website page to a T-shirt or an animated screen.

06 / Outcome

Delivered across web and print.

The 2022 competition used the owl identity across its web presence and event materials. I created a connected set of original graphics, animation and website experiences, combining visual identity, practical web implementation and usability considerations.

The work was completed for the 2022 event. This case presents the actual project deliverables, without inventing conversion or engagement metrics.
