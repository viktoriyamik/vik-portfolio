01 / Project goal

Goal

The goal of this project was to develop a strong visual identity and branding for the SAKUstars 2022 competition, with a focus on creating a memorable digital presence. My digital painting, Owl, was selected as the official visual symbol, representing the competition while contributing to its overall branding and graphic direction.

From the competition logo to the WordPress website, the identity needed to feel coherent across print, merchandise, social media and event communications.

02 / Research & concept development

Research & Concept Development

To ensure a cohesive and recognizable brand identity, I worked closely with the main project manager, conducting a personal interview to determine the competition’s graphic direction.

This process led to the selection of a lilac-based color palette, complemented by green, yellow, blue, and black, creating a visually striking and unified design system.

The visual identity needed to feel creative and welcoming to Finnish vocational students while remaining clear and consistent across formats.
03 / My key contributions

Designing across every touchpoint.

My role combined visual identity and UI/UX, web design, usability testing, and production of the event's print and digital materials.

Branding & Identity Design

Designed the competition logo, color scheme, official owl visual identity and supporting graphic assets.

Print & Digital Media

Created posters, postcards, banners, price tags, stickers and merchandise prints for T-shirts, hoodies, caps, bags, sunglasses, and water bottles.

Social Media & Animation

Created custom graphics and animated content for the SAKUstars 2022 website, Instagram and Facebook, including award-ceremony animations.

Presentation & Document Templates

Designed official PowerPoint and Word templates so the event team could communicate with a consistent look.

Web Design & Development

Designed and developed the WordPress website, sakustars.fi/2022, to provide competition details and updates with a clear information hierarchy and responsive layouts.

UI/UX & Usability Testing

Worked on navigation, readability, responsive behaviour and usability of the website to help participants and visitors find important information across devices.

04 / Challenges

Balancing creativity and consistency.

One of the main challenges in the SAKUstars 2022 project was balancing creativity with brand consistency. The visual identity had to remain cohesive across multiple platforms without losing the energy of the competition.

Creative consistency

Maintaining a unified visual identity across print materials, digital media, merchandise and event graphics.

Print and digital production

Adapting designs for different formats required attention to technical specifications, dimensions and colour reproduction.

Team collaboration

Integrating feedback from a diverse team while keeping a coherent creative direction and working closely with the main project manager.

A broad student audience

Creating an identity that resonated with Finnish vocational college students with varied interests and visual preferences.

Responsive WordPress design

Making the website visually engaging, functional and easy to use on both desktop and mobile devices.

Engagement across channels

Connecting the experience across physical materials, social media, website updates and the live competition.

These challenges helped me refine my creative and strategic thinking and deliver a cohesive experience across many touchpoints.
