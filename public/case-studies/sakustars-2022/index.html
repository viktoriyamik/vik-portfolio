01 / Project overview

One event. One recognisable identity.

The national SAKUstars cultural competition took place from 26 to 28 April 2022 in Tuusula, Finland, hosted by Keuda. Its theme was Vanhassa vara parempi (“Reusing the old is better”).

The goal was to build a strong visual identity and brand for the event, with particular attention to a memorable, usable digital presence. My digital painting Owl was selected as the official visual symbol and helped shape the overall graphic direction.

The design needed to work consistently across the event website, social channels, print materials, physical spaces and animated ceremony graphics.

02 / Research & concept development

Finding the visual direction.

To develop a cohesive and recognisable identity, I worked closely with the main project manager. I conducted a personal interview to clarify the competition's graphic direction, communication needs and constraints.

We developed a lilac-led colour palette, supported by green, yellow, blue and black. The owl illustration, distinctive event graphics and repeated use of these colours created a connected visual language.

The visual identity had to feel creative and approachable for vocational students while remaining consistent enough for organisers to use across many formats.

04 / Challenges

Creativity with real-world constraints.

Brand consistency

Maintaining one recognisable design across many platforms while still allowing room for creativity.

Production requirements

Adapting the identity to print sizes, colour specifications, merchandise and digital display formats.

Team collaboration

Incorporating feedback from a diverse project team while preserving a unified design direction.

Student audiences

Creating a visual identity that could appeal to vocational college students with varied interests and preferences.

Responsive web design

Making the WordPress website both visually appealing and functional across different devices.

Cross-channel communication

Connecting visitors and participants through on-site materials, print, event updates and social content.

These challenges strengthened my ability to connect design craft, research, collaboration and implementation instead of treating each format as a separate assignment.

07 / Impact & outcome

A cohesive identity across web, print and motion.

The SAKUstars 2022 event used the owl identity on its website, printed materials, merchandise, social graphics and ceremony displays. These delivered assets helped create a consistent and recognisable experience for competition participants and visitors.

This project brought together my UI/UX, branding, graphic design and web development skills while working with the event team. I present the completed work and its real-world applications without claiming unmeasured improvements in engagement or recognition.
