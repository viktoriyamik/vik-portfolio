01 / Product opportunity

Cleaning advice exists everywhere. Confidence does not.

When a cleaner encounters an unfamiliar stain, material or chemical at work, scattered search results may not show which advice is credible or safe. The product problem is helping them understand the next action without making them guess whether a source is trustworthy.

How might we help cleaners find an answer they can trust, understand the next safe step and know when to seek professional help?
02 / Research framing & prioritization

Begin with a high-value real task.

I compared candidate journeys, including professional learning, mentorship and on-the-job troubleshooting. The unfamiliar-problem journey became the main design path. Sofia, a developing cleaner, is a provisional persona used to focus the concept—not a verified representative research result.

03 / Information architecture

One primary task, several routes forward.

From describing a coffee-stain problem, Sofia can inspect results across formats, open a credited guide, check practical safety information and decide whether she needs expert support. Search, AI-assisted discovery and human routes remain available if the first answer is insufficient.

04 / Wireframes & prototype

From first structure to interactive guidance.

Paper sketches established information hierarchy before digital wireframes and the mid-fidelity interaction. The prototype keeps photo, text and voice options visible, distinguishes content types in results and provides a route to expert-backed advice.

05 / Feedback & iteration

Trust must be visible in the interaction.

Peer review and a prototype walkthrough revealed that adding a photo needs immediate feedback because users may show a stain before describing it in words. I revised the interaction to make attachment state visible and removable, and strengthened source visibility beside guidance.

The available evidence documents peer feedback and illustrative observations; it does not establish a representative live user study or measured product improvement. Next I would test the path with practicing cleaners.
