From too many choices
to quiet confidence.

A mobile-first AI styling concierge that understands the occasion, curates considered options and connects clients to a human boutique advisor.

AURA Atelier explores an AI-assisted luxury experience without replacing human judgment, from personal context to curated styling and boutique handoff.

01 / Strategic challenge

Luxury discovery is about confidence, not only products.

For a special occasion, more choices are not necessarily more useful. Luxury clients may be balancing social expectations, personal taste and uncertainty. I framed AURA around the client's desired feeling and occasion rather than beginning with a product catalogue.

AI helps articulate preferences and narrow options; a boutique advisor brings real expertise when context and taste need human judgment.
02 / Research framing

Design for the emotional job.

The priority persona Léa Moreau is a design hypothesis for a luxury client preparing for an important social event. Her underlying need is to feel confident and authentic, not simply to browse dresses. I proposed interviews, observation and competitor analysis to test assumptions about emotional context, personalization and trust.

03 / Service journey & MVP

Every step should make the next one easier.

The journey moves from discovering AURA to expressing a personal occasion, comparing a curated shortlist, giving feedback, connecting with a human advisor and continuing into the boutique experience. I prioritized conversation clarity and human continuity over unnecessary automation.

04 / UI and interaction

A quiet interface that stays out of the way.

Restrained colours, editorial typography and a short sequence of questions help the user focus on one decision at a time. The prototype makes recommendation rationale visible and creates a natural path to a human advisor.

05 / Human–AI interaction

Trust means knowing when to hand off.

The assistant should not silently take control of a consequential choice. Users can understand why looks are suggested, adjust preferences and choose whether to involve a human boutique advisor. The handoff is part of the service, not an error state.

06 / Test plan

Validate confidence before conversion.

AURA is a design concept with a prototype and proposed usability test plan. The planned evaluation checks whether users understand recommendations, can adjust them and feel confident about the handoff. I have not presented planned research as completed research or reported outcomes.
