01 / Design challenge

Finland’s first-of-its-kind direct-cleaner marketplace.

HaeSiivooja was designed around a specific difference: a customer can select an independent cleaner and initiate a booking request directly, rather than purchasing an agency-managed clean or first collecting competing offers. Customers still need to understand the work, price and confirmation status; cleaners need to manage their own services, availability and incoming requests. Both journeys share a booking without forcing both people into one interface.

The defining design choice was a cleaning-specific SaaS marketplace—not an agency supplying its own staff. I shaped the product, UX/UI, information hierarchy and the customer and professional journeys as one connected service.
How the models differ: Freska delivers a managed cleaning service using its own cleaners; Puhtaudella.fi gathers quote requests and providers respond with offers; HaeSiivooja is designed for direct selection of an independent cleaner and a booking request in the app. Direct does not mean automatically confirmed.
02 / Product thinking

Reduce uncertainty at each decision.

My decisions came from the different information each role needs: customers need scope, timing and transparent costs; professionals need clear requests, availability and work status.

01 / Scope

Make scope tangible

Separate service types from optional extras and show a readable summary. Customers can express a specific need, while cleaners can better understand the work requested.

02 / Coordination

Connect two workflows

Keep a shared booking request, but show different actions for customers and cleaners. A requested appointment must remain visibly different from confirmed work.

03 / Next steps

Explain value and fees

Separate free registration, the customer booking fee, cleaner subscriptions and the independent price of the cleaning job, so users know what they pay for and to whom.

Customer journey

Choose a cleaning type → select a provider and time → review details → track confirmation.

Cleaner journey

Set services and availability → review requests → accept work → manage the appointment.

03 / Visual identity evolution

Friendly enough to remember. Clear enough to trust.

Cleaning is a personal service. I wanted the identity to feel approachable without sacrificing the clarity customers and professionals need when discussing work and money.

The mascot's bright cyan and yellow became selective accents. Dark ocean-teal anchors the headings, navigation and primary actions; pale sky backgrounds support cards and grouped content. This preserves the mascot's friendly personality while keeping important information readable.

06 / What I designed and delivered

A first-of-its-kind service model, designed around choice.

The result is a cleaning-specific SaaS marketplace designed to let customers choose independent cleaners directly and send a booking request, while those professionals manage their services, availability and work. It connects customer selection with provider coordination, rather than replacing cleaners with an agency-owned workforce or routing customers through a quote competition.

CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

From need to a clear request

Service categories, optional extras, provider discovery, booking details and visible next steps help users understand what they are arranging.

CLEANER EXPERIENCE

From profile to manageable work

Professional profiles, service areas, availability and request management support the people delivering the cleaning.

The website is live, the Android app is available in Google Play open testing, and an iOS version has been developed but not publicly released. The product is designed as a cleaning-specific, independent-provider marketplace rather than a cleaning agency.

The guiding design decision: make scope, pricing, status and responsibility understandable to both people at every step of the service.
