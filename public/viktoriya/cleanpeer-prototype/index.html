Coffee stain keeps coming back

Maria K.

After cleaning, the mark reappears as the carpet dries. What should I check?

Anna Virtanen · Verified expert

Residue can rise as the carpet dries. Avoid adding more water, check the carpet care instructions and let it dry completely. If the mark returns, ask a professional to assess it.

Help & Support

How do I solve a problem?

Open Solve a Problem, choose a tag or describe your situation, then press Solve. Select a guide from the results, review the Steps and Safety tabs, and tell us whether it helped.

How do saved guides work?

Press Save to My Problems in a guide. Press Save to My Problems again to open the saved list, or use Saved in the bottom navigation. You can reopen or remove a saved guide there.

Photo, voice, chat and booking

These are simulated interactions for testing. Add photo displays a sample attachment. Record voice adds a demo recording; its play button reads a sample description when browser audio is available. Chat and booking show demo confirmations.

Keyboard and navigation

Use Tab to reach controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Use arrow keys within a tab list, or Home and End to move to its first or last tab. Escape closes the menu. Back and Home give clear ways to return.
